Nightshelf

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Nightshelf ("the app", "we", "us") is a bedtime-story app for iOS. It is built to know as little about you as possible. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no third-party tracker, and no advertising. This policy explains the little that's left.

What stays on your device

  • Your shelf and progress — which stories you've opened, your place in each one, and your bedtime settings (voice, speed, sleep timer, ambient sound). These live on your device and, via Apple's private iCloud key-value storage, sync between your own devices. Nightshelf keeps no copy of them on any server.
  • On-device narration — the built-in iOS voice synthesizes every word locally. Nothing leaves your phone.

What leaves your device, and why

  • Cloud narrators — if you pick one of the six cloud narrators (Nova, Alloy, Fable, Onyx, Echo, Shimmer), the story text is sent to Nightshelf's voice server and on to OpenAI to be turned into audio, then discarded. It isn't stored, isn't used to identify you, and is never used for anything but reading to you.
  • Tonight's Story — writing a custom bedtime story sends your chosen setting, tone, length, and the optional detail you typed to Nightshelf's story server and on to Anthropic, whose Claude model writes the story. If a cover picture is made for it, that request goes to OpenAI. Both are discarded the same way. Please don't include personal details you wouldn't want sent.
  • The full list — two companies can receive your words: Anthropic, for writing stories, and OpenAI, for reading them aloud and drawing their covers. Neither receives your name, your email or any account — Nightshelf doesn't have those to give. Stories you read from the built-in library are never sent anywhere, because they're already on your phone.
  • Purchases — Nightshelf Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles the transaction and shares only the receipt with the app, so it knows you're a subscriber. You can manage the subscription anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Premium system voices

If you download Apple's Enhanced or Premium voices from iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices, that download and those voices are handled by iOS, not by Nightshelf.

Data sharing

We do not sell data, we have no data to sell, and we do not share anything with advertisers.

Children's privacy

Nightshelf is made for adults who read themselves to sleep, and it is rated 13+. It is not directed to children and is not part of Apple's Kids Category. The shelf is adult literary fiction — alongside the fairy tales there is a Mystery & Gothic shelf holding Dracula, Jekyll & Hyde, Poe and Lovecraft — so parents should treat it the way they would a bookshelf, not a children's app.

Nightshelf collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics, so there is nothing to collect from a child in the first place. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has sent us something through the story form, email us and we will delete it.
